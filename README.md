# 🏎️ Genesis Motorsports – Chassis Design

## 📌 Project Overview

This repository contains the **chassis design developed for Genesis Motorsports** as part of our student motorsport project.

The chassis was designed according to the **applicable motorsport rulebook requirements**, with focus on safety, structural strength, reliability, weight optimization, and overall vehicle performance.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Design a safe and rigid vehicle chassis.
* Follow the specifications and safety requirements mentioned in the rulebook.
* Provide adequate protection for the driver.
* Optimize the chassis structure to reduce unnecessary weight.
* Ensure proper mounting provisions for major vehicle components.
* Develop a practical and manufacturable chassis design.

## 🛠️ Design Considerations

The chassis design considers:

* Structural strength and stiffness
* Driver safety and protection
* Chassis weight
* Load-bearing capability
* Suspension mounting points
* Powertrain mounting provisions
* Ergonomics and driver accessibility
* Manufacturing feasibility
* Rulebook compliance

## 📐 Chassis Design

The chassis was modeled using **CAD software** to develop and evaluate the overall frame structure.

The design process involved:

1. Studying the applicable rulebook.
2. Identifying mandatory chassis requirements.
3. Developing the initial chassis concept.
4. Creating the CAD model.
5. Designing structural members and mounting points.
6. Reviewing the design for safety and manufacturability.
7. Finalizing the chassis design.
